# 场景词库

**用法**：下面每项后面的数字是出现次数，用来判断哪个写法更常用，数字越大越优先。

## 背景类型占比

| 类型 | 出现 | 占比 |
|---|---|---|
| 白棚（pure white / seamless void / cyclorama） | 75 | 31% |
| 星空（starfield / nebula / milky way） | 47 | 19% |
| 实景（山、雪、水、街、室内） | 44 | 18% |
| 纯黑（low-key） | 43 | 18% |
| 其他（渐变、暗背景、抽象色块） | 33 | 14% |

## 一、白棚（最常用）

最高频三句：`pure white background`(51)、`seamless white void`(22)、`european white cyclorama studio`(19)。

```
pure white background, seamless white void, subtle floor shadow, clean studio lighting, no floor line
```
要点：白棚最容易出「地板线」和「灰底」，负面固定加 `floor line, grey background, cluttered background`（三者分别出现 24 / 50 / 45 次，都是高频防错词）。带反射就写 `glossy white floor with a very faint reflection`(6)。

## 二、星空（第二常用）

```
pure white background, the milky way confined to the top quarter, a dense starfield of pale cyan, faint blue-green nebula haze, soft mist at the transition, the far stars fading into pale lilac haze, fully gone before it reaches her head
```
高频件：`faint blue-green nebula haze`(13)、`a dense starfield of pale cyan`(11)、`soft mist at the transition`(11)、`milky way`(5)、`faint pink nebula haze`(5)。
要点：星空只占上部，靠 `soft mist at the transition` 收边，不要用一条干净的线切断。

## 三、实景

```
mountain forest background, thin morning mist, the far ones fading into pale lilac haze, background melting to white haze
```
高频件：`mountain forest background`(10)、`thin morning mist`(10)、`background melting to white haze`(6)、`wet stone floor`(6)、`airy translucent haze`(9)。
要点：远处压淡，近处留细节，靠雾把背景推远。

## 四、纯黑

```
pure black background, low-key lighting, thin cool rim light tracing her silhouette on both sides, warm glow spilling onto the darkness, seamless blend between figure and background, gentle bloom on the highlights
```
高频件：`pure black background`(16)、`void black`(7)、`seamless blend between figure and background`(5)。
要点：黑要立得住靠轮廓光；`faint smoke/haze catching the beam`(5) 让黑不发死。

